06 Aftercare

Healing, simplified.

  • Blot gently with a clean tissue for the first few hours.
  • Keep the area dry for 7–10 days: no swimming, saunas or heavy sweating.
  • Apply the provided balm sparingly, only as directed.
  • Don't pick or peel. Let flakes fall away naturally.
  • Avoid makeup on the area until fully healed.
  • Leave the second-skin film on for the time advised, usually 3–5 days.
  • Wash gently with unscented soap and pat dry.
  • Moisturize thinly 2–3 times a day.
  • No soaking, sun or tight clothing on the area for two weeks.
  • Use SPF once healed to keep fine lines crisp for years.

07 Questions

Good to know

Does it hurt?

A topical numbing cream is used throughout, so most clients describe it as mild pressure or a light scratch. Fine line tattoos are generally among the gentlest tattoos to get.

How long does permanent makeup last?

Usually 1–3 years depending on your skin, lifestyle and sun exposure. A yearly color boost keeps it looking fresh.

Is there downtime?

Brows look darker for the first week before softening. Lips may be slightly swollen for a day or two. Most people return to normal plans right away.

Who shouldn't get permanent makeup?

It isn't recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, while using certain acne medications, or with some medical conditions. We'll go through everything at your consultation.
